Black Hole Information & Holography

How much of a spacetime’s geometry is encoded in entanglement on its boundary? I study how entanglement can be represented by curves in the bulk, and how regions called islands contribute to the entropy of a distant system.

Reading geometry from entanglement

Partial entanglement entropy assigns contributions to the entanglement of a larger region. In static, pure anti-de Sitter space, my collaborators and I represented contributions between boundary points by weighted geodesics through the bulk. In this construction, counting the threads that cross a bulk surface measures its area. Minimizing this count over surfaces homologous to a boundary region recovers the Ryu–Takayanagi prescription for its entropy.

How two regions share an island

Two regions considered together can have an island that extends beyond the islands assigned to either region separately. How should this extra part enter a measure of correlations between the regions? In two-dimensional holographic models, we studied how different assignments affect the balanced partial entanglement entropy and how they correspond to competing cross sections of the entanglement wedge.
